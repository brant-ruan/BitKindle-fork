# Gamma implementation and validation

lib/gamma.c supplies lgamma and tgamma for binary32, binary64 and Windows
binary64 long double. No signgam extension is provided (it is not a C99
requirement). The private gamma-core.h arithmetic/exponential/packing helpers
are source copies of this project's runtime kernels.

Positive log-Gamma uses Stirling's expansion through the B24 term at x>=64.
Smaller arguments are reduced by recurrence. Near x=1, a stable difference of
the Stirling expressions at 64+z and 64 is followed by subtracting
sum(k=1..63) log1p(z/k). All terms of this difference retain their factor z;
subtracting two large rounded log-Gamma values would lose accuracy at the zero.
The recurrence also preserves the zero at x=2. The inverse-power differences
are propagated explicitly, rather than subtracting rounded powers.

Negative arguments use the reflection identity with a sine of the exact
fractional part of |x|, evaluated with split pi and a two-component Taylor
series. log-Gamma returns log(abs(Gamma)); the Gamma sign follows interval parity.
For tiny arguments a recurrence around one avoids reflection cancellation.
For very large positive log-Gamma arguments, scaling x before multiplication
avoids intermediate overflow. tgamma exponentiates the unrounded log-Gamma
result and packs the final magnitude directly, including subnormals.

Exactly representable small factorials have a separate exact path. Extremely
small tgamma arguments use a reciprocal path with the directed correction for
Gamma(x)=1/x-EulerGamma+O(x). This includes powers of two below 2^-60 and general
inputs below 2^-120. The general-input threshold keeps the correction below a
nonexact reciprocal's distance to a rounding boundary. Existing flags and errno
are preserved; new pole/domain/range errors follow the declared C99 policy.
Signed zero poles, negative integer poles, infinities and NaNs are handled before
the finite computation. Signaling NaN payloads are quieted with invalid.

Mathematical sources:
- https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11 (Stirling expansion)
- https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.5 (recurrence/reflection)
- https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg14/www/docs/n1256.pdf (7.12.8.3/4, Annex F)

make-gamma-reference.ps1 writes C test source only. It constructs sqrt(pi) from
Machin's formula with BigInteger arithmetic, then evaluates half-integer Gamma
by exact rational recurrence and log(abs(Gamma)) by an independent logarithm
series. Additional near-one/two and negative-pole fixtures use the local Gamma
expansion through its quadratic term. Their displacements are 2^-21 for float
and 2^-50 for double, so the omitted cubic terms are much smaller than a result
ULP for these particular checks; this does not prove all rounding decisions.
GAMMA-ORACLE.txt records the fixture comparison at 600 and 700 decimal places.

GAMMA.txt reports checks for 1,432 input/operation/precision records in all four rounding
modes, with every result bit checked; binary64 fixtures also call long double.
Three linked executions use both compiler generations and a mixed provider.
Additional tests cover recurrence, exact factorials, tiny reciprocal directions,
signed underflow, largest-input log-Gamma overflow, poles, special values and
preservation of prior error state. All object/executable production uses only
the existing custom compiler and linker.

Selected exact matches are not a universal correct-rounding proof. No full-range error bound or exhaustive coverage of negative
noninteger inputs and rounding-boundary cases is claimed.
